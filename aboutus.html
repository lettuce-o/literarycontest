Our Goal

The Page Turners Short Story Contest was created by high‑school students who believe in the power of storytelling. Our goal is simple: to encourage middle‑school writers to express themselves, build confidence, and discover the joy of creative writing.

We want students to feel proud of their ideas, excited to share their stories, and supported by older peers who understand what it's like to grow as a writer.

Who We Are

We are a team of Grade 10–12 students from West Humber Collegiate Institute who are passionate about reading, writing, and helping younger students grow. Each of us has experience with creative writing, English coursework, or peer mentorship — and we're excited to bring those skills to this contest.

Our mentors read every submission, provide supportive feedback, and help select the stories that will be recognized and published. We believe that every student has a story worth telling.

Meet Us

Chiara

Chiara is a senior student who loves creative writing, visual design, and helping younger students find their voice. She specializes in narrative structure and character development, and she helped design the look and feel of this entire contest website.

Pavith

Pavith is passionate about storytelling, especially fantasy and sci‑fi. He brings a thoughtful eye to world‑building and plot clarity, and he enjoys encouraging students to take risks and explore new ideas in their writing.
