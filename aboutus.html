The people behind the pages

About Page Turners

A student-led contest built on a simple belief: every young writer deserves a welcoming place to begin.

Our mission

Help young voices find their way onto the page.

Page Turners encourages middle-school writers to explore their ideas, grow their craft, and share original stories with a supportive audience. We want every entrant to feel proud of trying, whatever their experience level.

How it started

Why we started Page Turners

We started this contest because stories can build confidence, curiosity, and connection. As high-school students who love reading and writing, we wanted to make a friendly bridge between younger writers and older student mentors. Page Turners gives students a real prompt, a real audience, and encouragement to keep creating.

Our team reads submissions with care and aims to make the experience welcoming, constructive, and fun.
